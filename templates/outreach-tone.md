# Agency Outreach Tone Guide

## Voice
- Direct, confident, founder-to-founder energy
- No buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "holistic")
- Specific — reference something real about their company
- Short — cold emails max 5 sentences, follow-ups max 3

## Structure (cold outreach)
1. Hook: one thing specific to them (recent launch, funding, growth signal)
2. Problem: name the pain point we solve
3. Proof: one concrete result or example
4. CTA: one low-friction ask (15-min call, reply with a question)

## What to avoid
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- Generic compliments ("love what you're building")
- Listing all our services
- Asking for their budget in the first message

## Follow-up cadence
- Day 0: First outreach
- Day 4: First follow-up (add value — article, insight, not just "checking in")
- Day 10: Second follow-up (offer to help with a specific thing)
- Day 20: Breakup email ("closing the loop")

## Subject line formulas that work
- "[Company] + [specific problem we noticed]"
- "Quick question about [thing they care about]"
- "Saw your [recent thing] — had a thought"
